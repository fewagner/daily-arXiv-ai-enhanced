Abstract: The branching fractions and quasi-two-body $C\!P$-violating asymmetries of intermediate states obtained through an amplitude analysis of the charmless three-body decay $B^+ \!\to K^+ π^+ π^-$ are reported. Abstract: An amplitude analysis of $B^+ \!\to K^+ π^+ π^-$ decays is presented, based on $pp$ collision data recorded with the LHCb detector in 2011--2012, corresponding to an integrated luminosity of $3\,\text{fb}^{-1}$.
